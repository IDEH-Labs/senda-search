Verifiable Privacy Policy

In Senda, privacy is an architectural constraint. We do not profile users, log searches, or track behavior.

Commitments

  • No Query Logs: Searches are processed in memory and never stored on disk.
  • No IP Logging: Client IP addresses are never recorded in connection with searches.
  • Zero Cookies & Telemetry: No tracking cookies, no third-party analytics.
  • Referrer Protection: All responses enforce Referrer-Policy: no-referrer.

Content Removal

Webmasters wishing to exclude their sites from our index can specify it via robots.txt or contact IDEH Labs.
