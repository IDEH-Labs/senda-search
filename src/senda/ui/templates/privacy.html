Verifiable Privacy Policy

Privacy in Senda is an architectural constraint, not an advertising claim. The system is designed with zero-knowledge operational principles.

What We Never Collect

  • No Query Logs: User search terms are processed in volatile memory for the lifetime of the request and never written to disk or audit logs.
  • No IP Storage: Client IP addresses are never recorded or associated with search activity.
  • No Profiling: Senda does not build user personas, behavioral graphs, or search history profiles.
  • No Cookies or Local Tracking Identifiers: Senda sets zero tracking cookies. Language selection is stored purely in client-side localStorage on your device.
  • No Third-Party Transmission: Queries and metadata are never shared, sold, or proxied to advertising networks or third-party brokers.

Data in Transit & Referrer Defense

Senda serves HTTP responses with Referrer-Policy: no-referrer. When you click on any search result link, your browser is instructed not to send the referring search URL or search terms to the destination website.

Takedown & Domain Purging

Site owners who wish to remove their domains or specific URLs from the Senda index can submit takedown notices or block SendaBot via RFC 9309 robots.txt. Takedown requests purge all matching records and FTS indices immediately.
