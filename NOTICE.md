## Ethical and Architectural Commitments
1. **Zero Tracking:** Senda does not profile users, track IP addresses, or store query search logs.
2. **Strict Politeness:** Senda strictly respects the Robots Exclusion Standard (RFC 9309).
3. **SSRF and Anti-Abuse Protections:** Senda includes built-in network guards prohibiting connections to private, link-local, loopback, and internal addresses.
4. **Third-Party Rights:** Senda indexes document metadata (titles, canonical links, bounded semantic excerpts) and does not unlawfully reproduce or pirate proprietary works or full-text mirrors.
